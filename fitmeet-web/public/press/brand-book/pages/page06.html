04 · Logo

Safe zone & sizing

MIN. CLEARANCE = X

Protected space

Always leave clear space around the logo equal to the height of the “X” from the wordmark (marked next to the icon). No other element — text, photography, page edge — should enter that zone.

Minimum sizes

Below these dimensions, the radar-line details become unreadable, especially in print.

Digital: 24px
Favicon / avatar: 64px
Print: 15mm wide

Recommended backgrounds

The gradient version of the logo works best on a fully black or fully white background. On photography or busy backgrounds, use the mono (black or white) version for legibility.
