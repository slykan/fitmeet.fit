Color usage

The 60 / 30 / 10 rule

Black and white carry most of the surface area (backgrounds, text). Green and cyan are accent colors — for CTA buttons, icons, highlighted numbers and links. Avoid large fields of solid green or blue, as they lose the energy of the gradient.

Contrast & accessibility

Signal Green and Meet Cyan are light, saturated colors — text placed directly on them often fails WCAG contrast. Use them as accents on black/white backgrounds, not as a background for text.
