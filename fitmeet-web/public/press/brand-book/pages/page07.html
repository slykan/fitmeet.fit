05 · Logo

What not to do

The logo is one of the brand's most valuable assets — small changes undermine recognition and the sense of quality.

Don't stretch or distort proportions
Don't rotate the symbol
Don't change the gradient colors
Don't place on clashing backgrounds
Don't add shadows, outlines or effects
RANDOM TEXT
Don't pair with other wordmarks
Correct Use the supplied files unmodified, on recommended backgrounds, at the defined minimum sizes.
